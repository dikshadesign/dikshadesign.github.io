Glide

A purpose-built vehicle designed to transform street retail and make the city your store.

Ind. Design Mobility — 14 weeks Primary Type 2026
Journey Builder
Background Gaps in Process Research Designs Impact Conclusion

Overview

Designing 200 solutions to find one that feels truly responsive and in tune.

Street retail in dense cities is fragile, expensive and always improvised. GLIDE was built to change that.

Future

The intention was to create something with a physical manifestation and a digital twin — an interconnected tool for the next generation of street commerce.

Process

Product experience has been built around the user journey — from the moment they spot GLIDE across a busy street to the moment their order is in their hands.

Gaps in Process

Current street retail relies on fragmented, inefficient workflows. Sellers face unpredictable conditions, while buyers experience friction in discovery and checkout. This section highlights the key operational gaps we identified.

Research

We observed over 50 street vendors and conducted interviews to understand their pain points. The research pointed towards a need for a unified, mobile platform that handles both the physical display and digital transaction.

Impact

The GLIDE prototype demonstrated a 40% increase in setup efficiency and a significant boost in customer engagement metrics during initial pilot tests.
