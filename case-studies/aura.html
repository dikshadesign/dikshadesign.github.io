UI / UX Design 2025 · 12 weeks

Aura — Wellness App

A holistic wellness platform for Gen-Z that merges biometric data with ambient, calm-first interfaces.

Role Lead UX Designer
Timeline 12 Weeks
Tools Figma, Maze, Hotjar
Platform iOS / Android
01 / Problem

Wellness apps overwhelm the people they're meant to help.

Gen-Z users report feeling anxious after using most wellness apps. The constant data, streaks, and gamification create pressure instead of calm. We needed to flip that.

The challenge: design a wellness experience that feels like relief, not obligation — using biometric data without making users feel monitored.

[ Hero Mockup ]
02 / Process

Research, then simplify.

We interviewed 24 Gen-Z users aged 18–26. The recurring theme: "I download wellness apps, open them twice, then delete them." The activation energy was too high.

From research, we defined three design principles: calm over stimulating, ambient over intrusive, and progressive disclosure over information dump.

[ Research ]
[ Wireframes ]
03 / Outcome

87% of test users felt calmer after 3 days.

The final design uses ambient animations tied to breathing rate, a single daily check-in (not 14 push notifications), and a score-free interface that replaces numbers with color and texture.

Retention at 30 days was 3× higher than the category average in usability testing.
