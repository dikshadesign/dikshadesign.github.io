02 Design Philosophy

Why industrial training transforms software architecture.

Digital systems designed without respecting physical hardware realities fail in high-stakes environments. Here is how I approach complex operational problems.

Principle 01

Physical Reality First

Software on the factory floor must survive oily gloves, ambient glare, high-vibration gantry mounts, and emergency stops. My industrial design background guarantees that screen layouts respect spatial ergonomics and immediate tactile affordances.

Principle 02

Systems Over Silos

A 3D CAD model, an optical inspection rig, a procurement bill of materials, and an assembly technician’s shift are not disconnected tasks. I design the end-to-end operational pipeline connecting all four into one coherent digital thread.

Principle 03

Vibe Coding & Working Prototypes

Why debate in static slide decks what can be tested in functional code? I prototype working tools with real data, state transitions, and keyboard shortcuts—validating latency, information density, and user ergonomics before engineering commits sprint cycles.
