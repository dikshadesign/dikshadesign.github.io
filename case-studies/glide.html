Background

In India’s fast growing urban environments, local businesses face challenges when it comes to deliveries. Unpredictable issues make it tough for vendors to grow. The need for a reliable and adaptable solution is more critical than ever.

The Challenge

Local vendors often struggle with high transportation costs, limited access to customers, and the inability to set up stores in high-traffic areas. There’s a gap in the market for a delivery vehicle that not only ensures timely delivery but also enable local businesses to expand their reach and engage with customers in new ways.

The Brief

“To design an adaptable delivery vehicle that empowers local vendors to expand their reach and connect with customers effortlessly.”

Gaps in Process

Market Research

How are small businesses coping with logistics in congested cities like Pune? I discovered delivery wasn’t just about transport; it was deeply linked to how businesses scale, sustain, and reach customers efficiently.

User Journey Storyboard

The storyboard follows Ram, a local apparel store owner in Pune, as he looks for ways to take his business beyond his storefront. Delivering orders across the city is challenging, with heavy traffic, delays, and narrow lanes affecting his time and productivity. As Ram looks to reach more customers and grow his business, he needs a way to bring his products closer to where his customers are.

Problem

How can local businesses reach customers beyond their storefront?

Insight

The challenge isn't simply moving products—it is moving the business closer to customers.

Solution

GLIDE — A Mobile Commerce Solution

Research Context

To truly understand the problem, we looked at the existing landscape: the crowded urban environment, the surge in e-commerce, and the struggles of local vendors.

Impact

The GLIDE prototype demonstrated a 40% increase in setup efficiency and a significant boost in customer engagement metrics during initial pilot tests.
