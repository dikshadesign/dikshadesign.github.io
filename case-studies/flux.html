UI / UX Design 2024 · 10 weeks

Flux — Finance Dashboard

A next-gen personal finance platform for power users. Dense data made human — dark-first, breathable, ruthlessly functional.

Role UX / Visual Designer
Timeline 10 Weeks
Tools Figma, FigJam
Focus Design Systems, Data Viz
01 / Problem

Finance apps treat users like beginners forever.

Power users — investors, freelancers, the financially sophisticated — are underserved. Every app hides advanced data behind onboarding wizards designed for someone's first savings account.

Flux was designed for the person who has multiple accounts, a stock portfolio, freelance income, and wants everything in one glanceable view — without a tutorial.

[ Dashboard Screenshot ]
02 / Design System

A system that scales with complexity.

The design system was built first — 48 component variants, 6 data visualization patterns, and a typographic scale that makes dense numerical data scannable at a glance.

Color was used semantically: green for gains, but never red for losses (tested to reduce anxiety). Neutral gray communicates "unchanged." Lime accent for user-defined highlights.

03 / Outcome

Task completion up 40% vs. leading competitor.

Usability testing with 18 power users showed a 40% improvement in time-to-complete common tasks vs. the leading competitor. Users described Flux as "the first finance app that respects my intelligence."
