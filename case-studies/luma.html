Industrial Design 2025 · 16 weeks

Luma — Smart Lamp

A circadian-aware desk lamp that learns your daily rhythms and adapts its light to support your natural energy.

Role Industrial Designer
Timeline 16 Weeks
Tools Rhino 3D, Keyshot
Deliverable Concept + Prototype
01 / Brief

Light that understands you.

Most smart lamps are glorified light bulbs with an app. Luma was designed from a behavioral premise: what if a lamp could become invisible — quietly adjusting without ever needing to be controlled?

The brief was to design a product that sits confidently on a desk, communicates intelligence without screens, and is beautiful even when off.

[ Product Render ]
02 / Form

Form follows behavior.

The body is a single aluminum extrusion that tapers at the top — physically communicating the direction of light flow. The diffuser is frosted borosilicate glass, warm to the touch.

The only interaction surface is a single capacitive ring at the base. One touch: on/off. Rotate: brightness. Hold: activate circadian mode. No app required.

[ Sketches ]
[ CMF Board ]
03 / Outcome

Designed to disappear.

Luma was shortlisted for the NID Design Challenge 2025. Peer feedback highlighted the coherence of the CMF and the intentional restraint of the interaction model — doing less, better.
